About Pradeep Lakhmani Fabric

Our Story

Welcome to Pradeep Lakhmani Fabric, your trusted destination for premium quality fabrics in Ayodhya. For years, we have been serving our valued customers with the finest collection of fabrics for all occasions.

Located in the heart of Ayodhya at Ghanta Ghar, Refuse Market Chowk, we pride ourselves on offering authentic, high-quality fabrics at competitive prices.

Our commitment to excellence and customer satisfaction has made us a household name in the fabric industry of Ayodhya.
